## Privacy Policy

### Summary

- Azkar Coach does **not collect, upload, sell or share** any personal data.
- Everything you enter stays **on your phone only**.
- There is **no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking**.
- The app works fully offline.

### 1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following **only in your phone's local storage**. The developer cannot see it and it is never sent to any server.

| Data | Why it is stored |
|---|---|
| Your first name (optional) | To greet you on the Home screen |
| Your goals (azkar sets, tasbih targets, reminder times) | To show your goals and send reminders |
| Daily progress, streaks and qaza (missed days) | To show your progress and history |
| Mood check-ins (e.g. "calm", "anxious") | To show a matching note and a gentle weekly message |
| Duas or surahs you write yourself | So you can add them to your goals |
| Settings (language, Arabic text size, theme, vibration, quiet hours) | To remember your preferences |

You are in control of this data:

- **Delete everything:** uninstall the app, or go to *Android Settings → Apps → Azkar Coach → Storage → Clear data*.
- **Delete a single goal:** open the goal and tap *Delete goal*.

### 2. Information we collect

**None.** The app has no servers and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. No data is collected automatically or manually, and none is shared with third parties.

### 3. Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To remind you of your azkar and tasbih goals at the times you choose. You can turn this off at any time in Android settings. |
| Vibration | For tap feedback while counting tasbih. You can turn it off in the app's Settings. |

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or phone calls.

### 4. Links

If you tap a link in the app (for example to this policy), it opens in your web browser. That website's own privacy policy then applies.

### 5. Children

Azkar Coach is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

### 6. Security

Your data never leaves your device through the app. It is protected by your phone's own security (screen lock, device encryption). If you share your phone, others who can unlock it may be able to see the app's contents.

### 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same link with a new effective date. Major changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes on Google Play.
